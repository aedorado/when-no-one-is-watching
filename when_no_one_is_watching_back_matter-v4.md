### **His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda**
His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda is the Founder-Ācārya of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). In 1965, at the advanced age of sixty-nine, he traveled from India to the United States on a cargo ship to fulfill the sacred order of his spiritual master, Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura. With only a few rupees and a trunk of his translated *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* volumes, he successfully carried the cooling nectar of *kṛṣṇa-kathā* to a world burning in material anxiety. Within a decade, he established over a hundred temples, translated dozens of volumes of Vedic literature, initiated thousands of sincere disciples, and traveled around the world fourteen times. His uncompromised dedication, revolutionary literary legacy, and pure, loving heart remain the eternal foundation and supreme shelter of the disciplic succession.

### **His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami**
His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami (Kṛṣṇapāda) (1950–2005) (beloved spiritual master, pioneer preacher, and spiritual father) was an uncompromised spiritual warrior who served the mission of Śrīla Prabhupāda with absolute, fearless dedication. Born in Cleveland, Ohio, as John Favors, he graduated from Princeton University before being personally initiated by Śrīla Prabhupāda and later awarded sannyāsa, going on to become the highest-ranking African-American leader in ISKCON and a senior member of its Governing Body Commission (GBC). He took incredible, life-threatening risks to distribute books behind the Iron Curtain in the Soviet Union and war-torn Yugoslavia, and established flourishing devotee-centered communities, schools, clinics, and farms across West Africa. He authored numerous books, including his well-known *Spiritual Warrior* series, applying Kṛṣṇa conscious principles to the everyday struggles of the heart. His life was a testimony to selfless care, counseling thousands and devoting his last breath—even through his own final battle with cancer—to protecting the bodies and souls of the Vaiṣṇavas.

### **In Memoriam: The Caring Father**
This book is dedicated to the glorious, eternal legacy of His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami (Kṛṣṇapāda), a spiritual warrior whose life defied all material odds. From preaching behind the Iron Curtain under Yugoslavian imprisonment and state police surveillance, to building self-sufficient communities in West Africa amidst extreme physical austerities, Gurudev modeled what it means to be a "transparent postman" of the disciplic line.

His mystical ability to read the hearts of his disciples, his strict prohibition of corporate transactions in favor of absolute devotee care and spiritual security, and his final, sweet phone-call blessing—*"Don't come. Stay there... wherever you go, people will see me in you"*—remain the beating heart of this book.

### **His Holiness Bhakti Dhīra Dāmodara Swami**
His Holiness Bhakti Dhīra Dāmodara Swami is a sannyāsi and initiating spiritual master within ISKCON, who travels tirelessly to preach the message of the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* across India, Africa, and the world. As a disciple of His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami, he represents the uncompromised standard of loving care, bold preaching, and intense sādhana that he received from his spiritual master. He is dedicated to building self-sufficient devotee-centered educational communities, schools, and clinics, ensuring that the legacy of his predecessors is practically carried forward to protect the future generations of Vaiṣṇavas.
